# DCC Round 6 — Settings + RC Polish

This round adds a Settings panel and tightens release-readiness structure without changing destructive-operation behavior.

## Included
- Sidebar Settings section.
- Safety policy summary.
- Diagnostics and Refresh Drives shortcuts.
- Reset UI Preferences action.
- Clean/Format log copy shortcuts.
- Release-readiness summary cards.
- Ctrl+3 shortcut for Settings.
- Audit checks for the new Settings/RC surface.

## Safety stance
No clean, scan, or format policy was loosened in this round. Formatting remains removable-drive only and still requires the typed drive-ID challenge.
